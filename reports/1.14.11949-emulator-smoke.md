    Fatal signal 4 (SIGILL), code -6 (SI_TKILL) in tid 3804, pid 3804

The native crash report identified:

- Guest architecture: `arm64` running via translation on an x86_64 emulator.
- `libloader.so + 0x830BEC` — top guest backtrace frame.
- `libloader.so + 0x830D98` — previous guest frame.
- Next frame: Android ARM64 dynamic linker `call_constructors`.
- Host backtrace includes `tgkill`, confirming delivery of SIGILL through the translated runtime.

Android recorded the process death at `18:36:19.026Z`. Neither sample at 15 seconds nor at 45 seconds found a running game process. The screenshot captures show the state *after* the crash; they do not prove a playable menu.

**Interpretation:** failure occurs during native library initialization, before gameplay; there is not enough evidence to distinguish an intentional loader abort, ARM-translation incompatibility, or another initialization error. This result does **not** prove a crash on real ARM64 Android hardware, including the POCO X6.

**Next:** statically disassemble the two `libloader.so` regions and investigate ARM64-specific initialization behavior using unmodified binaries. No anti-cheat bypass or modified APK is involved.

The workflow result is a diagnostic **failure**, not a claim of stable gameplay or ESP.
