the environment had problems with graphics and Google-dependent services.

timing and prior errors do **not** prove the specific cause of the protection response.

In the two text logcat streams, the exact user-visible security toast
text was not present within the original game PID logs; prior original
screenshots showed the warning. Missing warning text in logcat does NOT
contradict those screenshots.

## Google Play environment limitations

The current Redroid installation does not have Google Play Services,
the Play Store or GSF. Standard Android app permissions cannot supply
those packages, a Play-installed license or a Play Integrity verdict.

Google's official documentation explains that Play Integrity can
distinguish recognized apps, genuine device integrity and a
special virtual-integrity label available for Google Play Games on PC.
No integrity token was accessed or verified in these experiments.

Sources:
- https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity/verdicts
- https://developer.android.com/studio/run/managing-avds

A separate, *unmodified* official Google Play Emulator image is a
legitimate compatibility alternative for the next probe. Having Google
packages installed alone is not sufficient evidence of certification,
licensing or Oxide gameplay.

## Conclusion

What is validated: process PID, initialization steps, background
service warnings and actual self-termination. Not validated: security
root cause, actual multiplayer boot or a working ESP. This experiment
requires no repeat of the full ~867 MB asset download.
